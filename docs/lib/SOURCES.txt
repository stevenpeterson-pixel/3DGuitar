Score sources for the song library
==================================

The compositions below are in the public domain in the United States (published 1930 or earlier).
The note data in scores.js was converted from these published transcriptions. Where a transcription
carries a Creative Commons licence, the licence and source are credited here as it requires.

From the Mutopia Project (https://www.mutopiaproject.org), converted from the MIDI rendering of each score:
  Lágrima (F. Tárrega), two-guitar setting, merged ........ Public Domain
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/TarregaF/lagrima-duo/
  Adelita (F. Tárrega) .................................... CC BY-SA 2.5
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/TarregaF/adelita/
  Recuerdos de la Alhambra (F. Tárrega) ................... CC BY-SA 3.0
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/TarregaF/recuerdos/
  Study in B minor, Op. 35 No. 22 (F. Sor) ................ CC BY-SA 3.0
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/SorF/O35/sorf_op35_no22/
  Maple Leaf Rag (S. Joplin), reduced here from piano ..... Public Domain
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/JoplinS/maple/
  Spanish Romance (Anonymous), guitar ........................ CC BY-SA 2.5
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/Anonymous/spanish-romance/
  Prelude, Cello Suite No. 1, BWV 1007 (J. S. Bach) ......... Public Domain
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV1007/bwv1007/
  Bourrée in E minor, BWV 996 (J. S. Bach), lute ............ Public Domain
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BachJS/BWV996/bourree/
  Canon in D (J. Pachelbel), violin part .................... CC BY 3.0
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/PachelbelJ/CanonInD/
  Für Elise, WoO 59 (L. van Beethoven), two-guitar setting .. Public Domain
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/BeethovenLv/WoO59/fur-elise-guitar-duo/
  The Entertainer (S. Joplin), piano ........................ Public Domain
      https://www.mutopiaproject.org/ftp/JoplinS/entertainer/
  Changes made: notes assigned to guitar strings and frets; Maple Leaf Rag transposed up a semitone,
  folded into the guitar's range and thinned to at most four notes at a time.
  The Entertainer and Für Elise folded and thinned the same way; Für Elise merges both guitar parts.
  Canon in D: the violin part is set as the three-voice canon (entries two bars apart) over Pachelbel's
  ground bass, which was added here. Bourrée: the marked repeats are written out.

Melodies and chord symbols from published ABC transcriptions (transposed to guitar-friendly keys;
the accompaniment patterns are generated by this program from the chord symbols):
  Wayfaring Stranger, Wildwood Flower, Sweet Georgia Brown, Cielito Lindo
      John Chambers' ABC collection, https://trillian.mit.edu/~jc/music/abc/
  Red River Valley
      Colin Hume's ABC collection, https://colinhume.com/ABC.txt
  Man of Constant Sorrow
      Frank Nordberg, musicaviva.com (via the mirror in John Chambers' collection)
  St. James Infirmary
      Thornton Rose, 2002 (via the mirror in John Chambers' collection)
  Georgia on My Mind
      Ian Hayden's ABC files, https://github.com/ian-hayden/abc-music-files
  La Cucaracha (verse and chorus)
      Jack Campin's Nine-Note Tunebook, http://www.campin.me.uk/ (chord symbols added here)
  Take Me Out to the Ball Game (1908 verse and chorus)
      The Digital Tradition (abc_dtrad TAKEBALL); chorus chords follow John Chambers' transcription,
      verse chords added here

The other arrangements in the library were written for this program.
